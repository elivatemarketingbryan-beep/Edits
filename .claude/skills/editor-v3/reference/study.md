# Study: the reel behind Editor V3

Learned from "The Life Cycle of a Creator (pt. 2)" by Will Schafer (@willtschafer): https://www.instagram.com/p/DP39ckzDRot/ . Nothing of his is in this kit, only measurements and written descriptions. Watch it to see the look. Never reuse his footage, music or sounds.

1080x1920, 24 fps, 120.9 s, -14.2 LUFS. Read every 0.5 s for the whole reel and every frame around each transition. Colours and caption boxes were measured from full frames. The sound was read from spectrograms and band levels with the voice separated, not by ear.

## The idea in one line

A filmed backlit side-profile silhouette is the home base. The colour of the backdrop is the emotional chapter. Everything else leaves from the head and comes back to the head.

## Share of screen time

| What | Seconds | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Silhouette talking | 62 | 51% |
| Surreal black and white shots, blue pictures | 21 | 17% |
| Dark graphic screens (charts, typed text) | 16 | 14% |
| Black | 6 | 5% |
| Real black and white photos of people | 6 | 5% |
| Colour B-roll at the desk | 5 | 4% |
| Empty backdrop at the end | 3 | 3% |

Colour B-roll is rare, and that's why it hits. Only 5 s in 2 minutes.

## The silhouette

- Side profile, face pointing screen left in every shot. It never flips.
- It looks filmed: strong backlight, slow handheld drift, soft focus falloff, a hint of real ear and neck colour, and fine hair strands against the light. The hair outline is a large part of the look.
- It isn't flat black. The face is `#060b0e` to `#090b0f`, and the T-shirt picks up the backdrop colour as a soft fill.
- The mouth outline visibly moves, and teeth catch light at times, so lip sync still shows in the outline.
- The backdrop has a bright centre behind the head and a strong vignette. In the teal it falls off to darker blue-green at the bottom.
- Hard punch-ins inside a shot on tense lines: about 1.3x to 1.7x (7.0 s, 13.5 s, 35.29 s, 55 s). The same take jump cuts bigger.

## Type

- **Captions:** white, medium-weight neo-grotesque, very tight tracking, 1 to 4 words at a time. Small on purpose: a three-word caption is 238x43 px on the 1080x1920 frame, centred, at 78% of the height. Fade in and out over 2 to 3 frames. A cut-off line ends in a hyphen.
- **Date title:** white, heavy, all caps, tight tracking. 792x64 px on the 1080 wide frame, sitting at eye level inside the head, 37% of the height. Opens each chapter.
- **Words behind the head:** huge bold caps behind the silhouette, glowing. Either a hard pop (24.83 s) or typed on with a `|` cursor (64.75 s).
- **Typed screens:** chapter colour on black with glow and a `<` cursor. About one letter every 2 frames, slowly pushing in until it crops off frame.
- **No captions at the lowest moment:** 31.5 to 35.2 s is voice only.

## Transitions, frame by frame

**Title stretch and head push (0.96 to 1.46 s).** The title scales up and starts smearing sideways over 6 frames, one frame of radial pixel smear at 1.21 s, title gone. Then the head scales up over 5 frames until its black fills the screen. The next scene fades up from that dark. The same stretch again at 37.2 s.

**Head as a window (35.29 to 37.29 s).** Hard punch-in, subject crushed to pure black. A tiny title appears inside the head at 35.5 s and grows the whole time. From 36.62 s the next picture fades up only inside the head over 16 frames, while the backdrop stays. Then the title stretches and the shoulder slides off.

**Film burn (22.12 to 23.25 s, again at 82.54 s and 88.4 s).** One frame of a thin vertical scratch and a speck. One frame of teal wash with the subject still visible. Two frames of full cyan. Then either the next shot, or 0.67 s of hard black in total silence, then scratch, dark edge, cyan leak with the next date on it, hard cut to the new chapter. The cut lands mid-sentence.

**Pan off, the ending (116.95 to 117.5 s).** The camera moves and the head leaves frame left in 13 frames with motion blur. The backdrop holds alone for 3.4 s while the music fades.

**Hard cut to black (58.5 s and 94.96 s).** After the biggest question and after the emotional peak. 3.5 s and 2 s.

## Pacing

71 hard cuts by frame comparison, 0.6 a second. 89 of the 121 seconds have no detected cut. The detector undercounts the blue pictures, whose changes are too similar for it, so those were counted by eye. Fast only in bursts: blue pictures about 6 a second, photos 2 to 4 a second with a peak of 16. Longest shot 20.4 s.

Edit density falls as the meaning rises. Busy graphics early, one plain take at the end.

## Sound

- **Music and effects sit 3.5 to 8.6 dB under the voice for the whole reel.** Voice about -15 LUFS, bed about -20. Only the last take's speech band is cleaner, at about 17 dB.
- **Red:** bass notes pulsing about every 0.79 s, under everything.
- **Digital ticks over every counting number** (3.5 to 5.3, 9.9 to 12, 16.7 to 19 s), 1.5 to 15 kHz. These are the loudest high sounds in the reel.
- **Keyboard ticks on the typed screens,** about 8 a second, 5 to 15 kHz (27.6 to 30.7, 52.5 to 53.2 s).
- **Huge bass swell** under the worried question (19 to 22 s), cut dead by the film burn.
- **Film burn:** a sound in, total silence on the black, a whoosh up to 10 kHz coming out at 23.1 s.
- **Music proper** from about 27.5 s: a soft pad with notes at 400 to 1000 Hz.
- **Riser into the head window** (36.5 to 37.4 s), reaching 3 kHz and above, very audible.
- **Blue pictures:** heavy bass, music and digital noise up to 15 kHz (40 to 53 s).
- **Almost nothing around the stopping question** (53.7 to 58 s), then a rising sweep and the biggest hit at 58.5 s, decaying to 62 s.
- **Teal:** whoosh in at 62 s, soft notes, a rhythmic pulse from 68.7 s, full music at the turn (80 to 95 s), dead silence for 0.6 s at 95 s, then a beat with bright transients to the end.

## Script structure

A dated diary in the first person, present tense, in short fragments. Three dates, three chapters. A rule of three with a repeated line and numbers that move each time. A false win in the middle, a question that stops the video, then the real answer. The structure can be copied. The story must be your own.

## Translating to 16:9

The reel only supplies timing, look and structure. Everything is recomposed for 1920x1080, never cropped from vertical.

- Head right of centre, face pointing left, shoulder running off bottom right. The empty left side is the speaking room.
- Keep the head big: about 65 to 75% of the frame height.
- Captions centred under the chin line, about 82 to 87% of the height, cap height about 2.2% of the frame height.
- Words behind the head gain the most: they can run the full width either side of the head.
- Dark graphic screens stay centred, and charts get wider.
- Blue pictures and black and white photos are made at 16:9.
