# Intake — one message, numbered, defaults in brackets

Ask only what is still unknown. Example:

1. **The song** — do you have one (send the file path), or should I make one for you for $0 on this computer
   [make one], or do you want to write the lyrics together first?
2. **Topic / message** — what is the song (and the video) about, in one sentence? [the song's own lyrics]
3. **Character** — the video stars CURSOR, a living orange text cursor that dances and lip-syncs. Keep CURSOR, or
   describe your own character (shape, colours, accessories) and I'll draw it in code [CURSOR]
4. **Look** — ten colour "worlds", one per song section (night terminal, paper studio, sky, sunburst pop, internet
   brutalism, gold rush, chrome voxel neon, warm sunset, all-worlds finale, sunrise) — or your brand colours [the worlds]
5. **Length** — the full song, or a 30–60 s cut for Shorts/Reels [full song]
6. **Format** — 16:9 YouTube 1920×1080 [16:9]
7. **Sound effects** — synthesised whooshes/impacts/clicks layered under the song, or the song only [both versions]
8. **Must-haves** — words, names, a URL, a call to action, anything that must appear [none]

Also say once: everything runs on their computer for $0; the only cost is Claude usage (a full song is a big build —
roughly 15–20% of a Max plan's weekly allowance with parallel builders; a 30–60 s cut is much less).

For a $0 song, also ask (or propose and confirm):
- genre + tempo (BPM) + vocal (e.g. "solo female, warm, melodic pop with sung-rap verses, 100 BPM")
- the hook line / title
- length (2:00–3:00 is the sweet spot)

Write the answers to `<project>/BRIEF.md` (song, topic, character, look, length, format, sfx, must-haves).
