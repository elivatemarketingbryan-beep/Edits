How a trial works

  1. Write 5–10 versions. A different hook on each, plus small changes in the body so Instagram doesn't flag them as duplicates. Claude writes them, or cuts them from a long-form video.
  2. Film once, edit once. Finish one version, then "Make versions from one reel" builds the rest from it.
  3. Post them all as trial reels. In Instagram, turn on "Trial" when you share. Log the date.
  4. After 16–24 hours, log the views and pick the winner. Re-upload the winner to your feed (don't tap "Share to anyone"), then farm it.
