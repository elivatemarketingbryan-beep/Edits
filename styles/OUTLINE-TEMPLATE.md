# Bryan's outline template (the "Angle, stories and real proof" box)

Every long-form video starts as this outline. When a `research` request runs, Claude fills it in the
video's `notes` (the "Angle, stories and real proof" box on the Plan page) **before** any script is
generated, using the research. Bryan's words (Bryan, 2026-11-14): "auto fill this before it generates
the script with the research and angles, it should be filled exactly as shown."

## Rules

- **Exactly this shape.** Same lines, same order, same labels, word for word: the four "How can I make this
  appeal…" questions, `Intro`, the four intro prompts, `1 Topic:`, `Act 1:` / `Act 2:` / `Act 3:` with
  `Open Loop` and `Payoff 1–3`, the last `Open Loop`, and `CTA: Link -> Curiosity Gap -> CTA/Promise`.
  Each label on its own line, its answer on the line(s) under it. Plain text, no markdown, no bullets.
- **Every slot filled.** No empty `Payoff 2:` or `Open Loop` left. Payoff labels get a short name
  (`Payoff 1: Missed-call text-back`), then one to four short spoken lines under it.
- **Bryan's lines stay.** Anything he already typed in the box stays in its slot, in his words (fix spelling
  only). Fill only what's empty, and keep his order of acts and payoffs.
- **From the research.** Use its numbers with their sources, its "Fix before filming" list (so no `[CLIENT]`
  lines, no unsourced stats, no borrowed last line), and its outside proof. Real proof only: Bryan has no
  clients, so proof is his dad's business, his own work, live demos, or named public sources. Anything that
  needs his data is `[PLACEHOLDER: what he must supply]`.
- **His voice.** Short lines he'd say out loud, like the filled example and his own outlines. The company is
  Elivate Marketing; the CTA points to a related video, then "book a call below".
- **Then the script.** The script (gate, then script) follows this outline's acts and payoffs in order.
