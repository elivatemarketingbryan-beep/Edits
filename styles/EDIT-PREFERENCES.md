## YouTube: motion-graphic heavy (2026-10-29, replaces "don't overdo the effects")

2026-10-29 · every 16:9 / YouTube video · Bryan wants a motion-graphic heavy edit: "I want a
lot more motion graphic heavy type of video." Something should be moving almost all the time:
a big graphic scene about every 30-40 s, punch-in cuts on the talking stretches (wide / close

Story stretches get scenes that act the story out. The old "about one big effect a minute" and
"leave it out when in doubt" rules below no longer apply; keep only "never the same effect twice
in a row" and "every graphic has a reason in what he's saying."

## YouTube (16:9 long-form): don't overdo the effects (SUPERSEDED 2026-10-29, see "motion-graphic heavy" above)

2026-10-28 · every 16:9 / YouTube video (long-form styles, youtube-clips) · Effects are an
accent, not the edit. A YouTube video is watched for the talk, so most of it is clean
talking head with good captions and cuts. (Bryan: don't overdo how often effects come up.)

- **Big effects** (any Video FX effect, full-frame graphic, B-roll takeover, transition
  effect): about **one a minute at most**, never two within 30 s, and none in a stretch
  where he's telling a story or making an argument; save them for the beats that earn one
  (a number, a list, a reveal, a chapter change).
- **Small moves** (a punch-in, a lower-third, a callout on screen): no more than one every
  ~20 s. Pixel zoom never runs "all the way through" on a YouTube video.
- **Same effect twice:** not within 3 minutes, and no single effect more than 3 times in
  one video.
- **Ideas and combos for YouTube:** pitch fewer, spaced-out ideas (roughly one per minute
  of video). No "max" / "Full send" combo; the levels are **light** (the 3-5 biggest
  beats) and **clean** (1-2 beats plus chapter cards). Every combo's pitch says how many
  effects in how many minutes, e.g. "5 effects across 8 minutes".
- **When in doubt, leave it out.** A plain talking stretch is fine; an effect with no
  reason behind it is not.

Short-form (9:16 Reels, TikTok, Shorts) is unchanged: heavy combos are welcome there.
