# Elivate Marketing colour palette

The one set of colours for every video, reel, caption, graphic, thumbnail and carousel. Exact values live in
`styles/brand.json` under `palette`, `captions` and `reels`; the picture is `styles/brand-palette.png`.
These come from the VSL look Bryan approved (`hf-graphics/g/vsl.css`).

## The colours

| Name | Hex | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#050308` | Default stage background, near-black with a purple cast |
| Midnight | `#1A0B38` | Edges of full-screen wipes, darkest card tint |
| Royal | `#3B1A75` | Middle of full-screen wipes, card tints |
| Deep | `#4C1D95` | Glows and card borders |
| Violet | `#6D28D9` | The flat glow along the floor of a stage |
| **Core purple** | `#8B5CF6` | **The brand colour.** Buttons, active rows, charts, underlines |
| Light purple | `#C4B5FD` | Key words and small labels on dark backgrounds |
| Mist | `#EDE9FE` | Pale tint on rare light backgrounds |
| Gold highlight | `#FFF3CF` | Top of the gold text gradient |
| **Gold** | `#E8C77A` | **Money and results only**, plus key words on the purple set |
| Gold deep | `#B8913F` | Bottom of the gold text gradient |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main text |
| Success / Loss | `#22A06B` / `#E5484D` | Only when green or red carries meaning |

## How to use them

**Captions**
- Main words are white, always.
- Key word on dark or neutral footage: Light purple `#C4B5FD`.
- Key word on Bryan's purple-lit set: Gold `#E8C77A`. Purple words vanish against the purple wall (his note, 2026-10-29), and so does red.
- Money and results are gold on any background.
- Soft dark shadow behind words; a dark pill (`rgba(5,3,8,0.72)`) when the footage is busy.

**Reels (9:16)**
- Stage: Ink with the Violet floor glow.
- Hook: white, with the punch word in the purple text gradient (white → light → core) on dark, or gold on the purple set.
- CTA button: Core purple with white text. Progress bars: Light purple.

**Graphics and themes**
- Purple is the structure, gold is the reward. Gold never decorates something that isn't money, a result or the one key idea.
- At most one accent per line of text.
- Glows go along the floor or in the corners, never a centred oval (EDIT-PREFERENCES).

**Gradients:** purple text `#FFFFFF → #C4B5FD → #8B5CF6`, gold text `#FFF3CF → #E8C77A → #B8913F`, wipe `#1A0B38 → #3B1A75 → #1A0B38`.
