Source: last 30 posts each from @mandothemarketer and @sparkmarketingagency.co (Apify scrape), every reel
transcribed with WhisperX large-v3. Raw data in Video Studio `projects/_ig-research/` (not committed).

## Length

| | @mandothemarketer | @sparkmarketingagency.co |
|---|---|---|
| Talking reels measured | 17 | 26 |
| Spoken words, median | 228 (middle half 111-328) | 165 (middle half 116-215) |
| Runtime, median | 53 s | 47 s |
| Speaking pace | ~218 wpm | ~222 wpm |

Both talk ~30% faster than Bryan (168 wpm) and cut tight, so **match word count, not seconds**:
teaching reels 200-330 words, short punchy reels 110-130 words. At Bryan's pace (before jump cuts)
that runs about 1:10-1:55 and 40-50 s.

- Spark's reels barely travel (100-2,300 plays); topics: too early for ads, CRM, film every project,
  sending conversion data back to Meta, ad fatigue, slow season, "lead problem vs system problem",
