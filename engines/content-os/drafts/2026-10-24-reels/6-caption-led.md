# Caption-led reels (10)

Shape: Caption-led. Hook (6-10 words) → 3-6 short lines, one thought each, exactly one [EMPHASIS] per line → closing line.
Short by design (20-35 s). Fitted captions = talking head with big fitted words; B-roll and stacked text = job-site B-roll with words stacked over it.

## T01 · Fast beats cheap
**Edit style:** Fitted captions · **Shape:** Caption-led · **Runtime:** ~0:25
**Hook:** "Homeowners don't hire the cheapest contractor. They hire the fastest."
**Beats**
1. Say each line as its own beat; the captions fill the frame word by word.
2. Pause half a beat after each emphasis word.
**Delivery:** Locked-off talking head, direct eye contact.
**Script**
```text
Homeowners don't hire the cheapest contractor. They hire the fastest. [HOOK]
The first one to text back. [EMPHASIS: first]
The first one to show up. [EMPHASIS: show]
The first one to send the estimate. [EMPHASIS: estimate]
Price is the last thing they compare. [EMPHASIS: last]
Be fast, and you'll rarely have to be cheap. [EMPHASIS: fast]

```
