# Caption-led reels (10)

Shape: Caption-led. Hook (6-10 words) → short punchy lines, one thought each, exactly one [EMPHASIS] per line → closing line.
Target 110-130 spoken words (matches the short reels of the tracked competitors). Fitted captions = talking head with
big fitted words; B-roll and stacked text = job-site B-roll with words stacked over it.
