# Proof reels (10)

Shape: Proof. The claim → each claim with what proves it (a [PROOF] tag) → what it means for the viewer → CTA.
Every proof is something Bryan can actually film: his own screens, public pages (Google, Meta Ad Library),
live searches, his guarantee. Tests marked "run it first" need the real numbers in `{}` before filming.
