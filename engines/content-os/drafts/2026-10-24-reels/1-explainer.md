Step one, every missed call triggers a text back within seconds. [STEP: 1]
Something simple, "Hey, sorry I missed your call, I'm on a job right now, what can I help you with?" [STEP: 1]
Step two, their reply lands in one inbox, not scattered across your phone, your email and your Facebook. [STEP: 2]
Step three, if they don't answer, a second text goes out the next morning so the conversation stays warm. [STEP: 3]
Here's why this matters. [EMPHASIS: speed]
78% of homeowners go with the first contractor who gets back to them. [NUMBER: 78%]
Not the cheapest. Not the best reviewed. The first.
So that missed call isn't a missed call, it's a job you handed to someone else.
That's not a marketing problem, that's a system problem.
