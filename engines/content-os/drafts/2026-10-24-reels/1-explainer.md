## E01 · Set up missed-call text-back before you run another ad

**Hook:** "Most contractors lose jobs on calls they never even hear ring."
**Beats**
1. Say the hook straight to camera, then name the moment it happens: up a ladder, on a roof, driving between jobs.
2. Explain why it matters: a homeowner who gets voicemail calls the next contractor on the list instead of waiting.
3. Walk the three steps of a missed-call text-back while the panel lights up each one, with the exact text on screen.
4. Land the 78% number as the reason speed wins, do the math on a lost call, then close on the follow CTA.
**Delivery:** Calm and flat, like you're showing a buddy something on your phone. Show your own phone receiving the auto text if you can.
**Script**
```text
Most contractors lose jobs on calls they never even hear ring. [HOOK]
You're up on a roof, you're driving to the next job, you're standing in someone's kitchen, and your phone goes off in your pocket.
You can't answer it. That's normal, you're working.
But here's what happens on the other end.
That homeowner doesn't leave a voicemail. Almost nobody does anymore.
They hang up, go back to Google, and call the next name on the list.
And that contractor picks up, books the estimate, and you never even knew the job existed.
So here's the fix, and it takes one afternoon to set up.
Step one, every missed call triggers a text back within seconds. [STEP: 1]
Something simple, "Hey, sorry I missed your call, I'm on a job right now, what can I help you with?" [STEP: 1]
Now they're not sitting in silence wondering if you got it. They're already talking to you.
Step two, their reply lands in one inbox, not scattered across your phone, your email and your Facebook. [STEP: 2]
So when you get down off that roof, every conversation is in one place, and you know exactly who to call first. [STEP: 2]
Step three, if they don't answer, a second text goes out the next morning so the conversation stays warm. [STEP: 3]
People get busy. They get distracted. That second text is what brings them back. [STEP: 3]
Here's why this matters. [EMPHASIS: speed]
78% of homeowners go with the first contractor who gets back to them. [NUMBER: 78%]
Not the cheapest. Not the best reviewed. The first.
So think about what one missed call is actually worth.
If your average job is $8,000 and you miss two calls a week, that's $16,000 a week in jobs you never even got to bid on.
That's not a marketing problem, that's a system problem.
```
