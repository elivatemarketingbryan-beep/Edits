# Relatable thought reels (10)

Shape: Relatable thought (AESTX pattern). Honest opener → what most people think → the honest reality →
"that's why X beats Y long term" → "Follow for more." No hard sell, no offer. Film while moving or in
real places (truck, desk, job site); keep the small pauses in. Target 45-60 s.

## R01 · I still overthink posting
**Edit style:** Personal reel · **Shape:** Relatable thought · **Bucket:** Posting nerves · **Runtime:** ~0:55
**Hook:** "I still overthink posting."
**Beats**
1. Say the hook like you're admitting it to a friend.
2. What most people think: once you're good at this, it stops feeling weird.
3. Honest reality: it doesn't, you just post anyway.
4. Lesson line, then "Follow for more."
**Delivery:** In the truck, phone propped on the dash.
**Script**
```text
I still overthink posting. [HOOK]
I'll film something, watch it back, and find ten reasons not to put it out.
My voice sounds weird. The lighting's off. Somebody's already said this better.
Most people think once you get good at this, that feeling goes away.
That the people posting every day just aren't nervous anymore.
Honestly, it doesn't go away.
You just get used to posting anyway.
You hit post before you can talk yourself out of it, and you move on with your day.
And the stuff I almost didn't post is usually the stuff people message me about.
The real stuff. The stuff that wasn't perfect.
Nobody remembers the video you didn't post.
That's why showing up beats being perfect, long term. [EMPHASIS: showing up]
Follow for more. [CTA]
```
