## Which account gets the post: the topic split

**The test: who is it for?** If a contractor gets value from it → contractor account. If someone who
wants to build what Bryan is building (start an agency, work for themselves, live with purpose)
gets value from it → personal account. Same event, two angles: a client's first booked job is "how
this roofer got 9 estimates from one campaign" on the contractor account, and "I landed my first
client, here's what that week looked like" on the personal one.

| Contractor account (@elivatemarketing.bryan) | Personal account (Notion: Personal Content Pipeline) |
|---|---|
| **Audience:** contractor business owners | **Audience:** people who want to start what Bryan did (future course buyers) |
| **Topic:** THEIR business growing | **Topic:** BRYAN's journey and life |
| Results: campaigns, jobs booked, cost per lead | Building from zero: first client, first $1k month, real numbers he's hit |
| Lead systems: speed to lead, follow-up, tracking | Day in the life: client calls, job-site visits, the workspace, the grind |
| Ads, SEO, Google, websites, explained plainly | Lessons from building: sales, hiring, mindset, mistakes running the agency |
| Sales and pricing for contractors: bids, estimates, value over price | Faith & purpose: why he builds, credit to God, serving over selling |
| Contractor life: trucks, crews, equipment, odd job requests | Family & roots: where he comes from, who he does it for |
| Behind the scenes: building a client's ad, site or tracking | Milestones & wins: real milestones only, with gratitude |
| CTA: follow for breakdowns / the offer keyword | CTA: follow, save, share. No offer until the course launches |

**Never crossed:** no cars, lifestyle or "my journey" posts on the contractor account; no contractor
marketing tips aimed at contractors on the personal account (lessons there are aimed at people
building an agency). Personal ideas go in the **Personal Content Pipeline** with a Pillar set; contractor
ideas go in the Content Database.

## Instagram — <<PERSONAL_IG_HANDLE>> (personal: the lifestyle of a marketer)

- **Goal right now: build an audience. Nothing is sold here yet.** Later it sells a course on how Bryan
  started his agency; that course doesn't exist, so it is never named, priced or teased as "coming
  soon" until he says it's launching.
- **Cadence:** 3-4 posts a week, mostly photo carousels and short vlogs.
- **What it posts:** a day running the agency, client calls and job-site visits, the workspace, faith,
  family and the story so far. The angle is **"building it from zero, in public"**: first client,
  first $1k month, first hire. Milestones only when they're real.
- **No offer CTA.** The CTA is follow / save / share. Exception to the one-CTA rule in CLAUDE.md
  until the course launches.
- **Models:** @scalewithmando and @sparkwithcesar (`competitor-list.md`). Mando: ~85% life and story,
  true milestones, faith and family. Cesar: agency "reality" talks plus lifestyle. On both, the
  breakouts are **short clips (7-20 s) with little or no talking** and a story in the caption; long
  agency talks get a few thousand views. Copy the mechanic, never their specifics (cars, "$500k/month")
  that aren't Bryan's.
- **Format mix:** about half short story clips (under 20 s), the rest photo carousels and an
  occasional 30-60 s "what I learned building the agency" talk.
- **At launch (later):** about 1 post in 7 becomes a comment-keyword lead magnet for the course
  (Mando's "Comment WAAS" posts drive thousands of comments each).
