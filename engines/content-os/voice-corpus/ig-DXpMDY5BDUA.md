The 3-part system it takes to make sure every lead that comes into your business actually
gets contacted, nurtured, and delivered to you ready to close.
Follow this page if you want more breakdowns like this.
Most contractors believe they have a lead problem.
They usually don't.
They usually have a lead management problem.
Leads come in, but they're getting lost, going cold, or never getting followed up with
properly.
And every single one of these is a job that you've already paid for that you're giving away
for free.
So this is a 3-part system that fixes it.
Part 1 you have lead capture.
When a homeowner fills out your form right now, what do you actually get?
A name and a phone number that you might see in 2 hours.
That's not capture, that's not notification.
Real lead capture gives you the complete profile the moment someone reaches out.
Their name, phone, email, the address, and what service they need and what page they were
on.
And even how they find you.
So when you call that lead back, you already know who you're talking to and exactly what
they need and why they reached out.
That one shift changes the entire quality of the first conversation.
Then you have lead nurture.
This is the part where most contractors lose jobs they didn't even know they had.
A homeowner submits a form, nobody calls for 2 hours.
By the time someone does, they've already talked to someone else and they're building trust
with a different contractor.
You are never their only option and the window before you move on is under 5 minutes.
Lead nurture means the moment a form gets submitted, the system responds automatically.
A text goes out in under 5 minutes and if no one answers then follow ups happen.
Part 3 is the lead delivery.
Even when you capture and nurture correctly, if the lead gets buried in a messy inbox or a
scattered set of apps, things will fall through.
Lead delivery means every qualified lead gets organized and handed to you clean.
You see who came in, where they are in your process, and what needs to happen next.
You're not guessing, you're not digging through old texts and emails trying to remember who
you called.
You have a clear pipeline that shows you exactly where every opportunity stands.
So you can focus on closing instead of managing chaos.
