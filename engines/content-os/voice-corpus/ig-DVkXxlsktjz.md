# IG reel DVkXxlsktjz — 803 plays

Most contractors struggle to get reviews, not because their work is bad, but because they
ask at the wrong time.
Right after the job is finished, the homeowner is excited.
They're looking at the finished product, they're imagining friends coming over, they're
thinking about how good the house looks.
Their emotion is going up.
And this is important.
Customer satisfaction follows a curve.
Right after the project is done, their excitement is building, and then it reaches what I
like to call the sweet spot.
This right here.
It's the perfect moment to ask for a review.
They're happy, they trust you, they just saw the finished work.
This is when getting a 5 star review is the easiest.
So here's what most contractors do.
They wait and they maybe send an email 3 weeks later.
The homeowner is busy and the review never gets written.
Ask for the review at peak emotion right when the homeowner is happiest with the work.
That moment right after they see the finished result is the exact window where reviews
happen.
Miss that window and most of the time you'll lose the review forever.
