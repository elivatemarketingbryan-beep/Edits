# Content System: Stories + Reels + YouTube

The planning rules `content-calendar` follows when it fills a week. **Bryan's own rules in
`platforms.md` win wherever they disagree with this file** (for example, no car or lifestyle posts on
the contractor account). Written for the contractor account (@elivatemarketing.bryan); the offer is
in `backbone/offer.md` and the default Story CTA is DM "LEADS" to book a meeting.

The Story rules are built in full (templates, render scripts and frame-by-frame structures) by the
`story-sequence` skill in the private `story-sequences` repo. This file only holds what the planner
needs to schedule them.

## The three pillars

| Pillar | How often | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Story sequences** | **Every day.** Not 5x a week: every day | Nurture and convert. Holds the weekly hard CTA. |
| **Reels** | Per `platforms.md` (default 1 a day; at least one every other day) | Attention and nurture. **No CTAs on reels.** |
| **YouTube** | Weekly long-form | Deep trust. This is where buyers decide. |

**Quality over quantity:** one weak post can undo months of trust.

## How the funnel fits together

1. **Top: attention.** Follower ads aimed at contractors (ICP followers roughly $2–6, profile visits
   under $0.40), plus reels with **disqualification hooks**: trade language that only contractors feel
   at home with. No chasing viral reach or vanity followers.
2. **Middle: nurture.** This is where trust gets built:
   - daily Stories
   - highlights as a credibility vault (client results)
   - YouTube
3. **Bottom: convert.** This is where leads turn into calls:
   - retargeting ads
   - inbound DMs
   - **one hard Story CTA a week**
   - DM setting
   - the call

**Pull, not push.** Marketing should do about 80% of the selling, so leads arrive pre-sold.

**Trust math:**
- About **7 hours** of watch time, **11 touch points** and **4 platforms** (Stories, reels, YouTube, DMs)
  before someone buys a high-ticket offer.
- It takes about **100 days** to compound.
- Each Story view moves someone about 1% closer to buying.

## Story calendar (put one row per day in `calendar.md`, platform `IG Story`)

| Day | Sequence | Slides | CTA | Build |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Client result: the number, then the story and impact behind it | 2–4 | — | native IG |
| **Tue** | YouTube CTA: proof screenshot → social proof → "I broke it all down" | **always 3** | soft → YouTube | native IG |
| **Wed** | Pattern interrupt / belief shift: how Bryan thinks and why he's different | 4–7 | **none** | native IG |
| **Thu** | **Hard CTA**: DM asset (screenshot it), DM keyword, book a call, or low-ticket | 3–5 | **hard** | high-quality (template) |
| **Fri** | Data-driven client win: before → after numbers over time | 3–4 | — | high-quality or native |
| **Sat** | YouTube CTA, story-led: tell the story first, then link | 3–5 | soft → YouTube | native IG |
| **Sun** | Day in the life / values. Keep it contractor-relevant on this account: job sites, crews, the work behind the scenes | any | **none** | native IG |

Rules for the Story calendar:
- **3 CTAs a week**: 2 soft (Tue and Sat to YouTube) and 1 hard (Thu).
- **5 native Instagram edits plus 2 high-quality ones.**
- Plan the copy, proof screenshots and photos ahead. Native Stories get posted on the day, in
  10–15 minutes. Never invent results or scarcity.
- **Story copy is in Bryan's voice** (`voice-dna.md`), with no AI phrasing.
- **One story across the slides.** Hook with a proof screenshot when one exists.
- **Wrap reels in a sequence.** Never post a bare reel to Stories: put a hook slide and a context slide
  in front of it.

## Reels

- Reels **never convert**: no direct CTAs. They bring the right contractors to the profile and warm
  them up; Stories and YouTube do the converting.
- A guide mix, which `platforms.md`'s weekly mix of 2 results / 2 teaching / 1 behind the scenes
  already fits:

  | Share | Type |
  |---|---|
  | **40%** | Authority / undocumented (behind the scenes, building a client's ad, on a job site) |
  | **30%** | Credibility / proof (real campaign numbers, jobs booked) |
  | **20%** | Value / problem-solving walkthroughs (screen-share fixing one contractor problem) |
  | **10%** | CTA (at most 1 in every 9–20 pieces; most CTAs live in Stories) |

- **Undocumented over documented.** Show the work instead of sitting down to "3 tips".
- **Speak the trade's language.** Keep naming the mechanism (the lead system / 2-step close).

## YouTube

- Instagram grabs attention; **YouTube converts it**. 20–30 minutes with Bryan builds more trust than
  any viral reel. Plan **one long-form a week**, and point Tuesday's and Saturday's Stories at it.
- The 4 formats (rotate them):

  | Format | Example |
  |---|---|
  | **Framework / tutorial / step-by-step** (converts best) | Full ad setup walkthrough for a roofer |
  | **Behind the results** | Podcast-style chat with a contractor client: before → bridge → after |
  | **A → B journey** | From zero leads to a booked-out month, every step |
  | **Who you are / where you're going** | Agency life, client visits, the mission (or on the personal account, if the test in `platforms.md` says so) |

## Gaps to flag (on top of the skill's own list)

- A day this week with no Story row.
- No hard Story CTA this week, or more than one.
- A Tuesday or Saturday Story CTA with no YouTube video to point at.
- A reel row carrying a direct offer CTA.
- No long-form planned this week.

## The loop

Follower ads + reels → profile + Stories → YouTube → DMs → call → close.
- Log where each client came from (which video, which Story) on the onboarding form.
