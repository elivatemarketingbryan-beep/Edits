# Reels + YouTube

How `content-calendar` plans **reels and YouTube**. Stories have their own, separate rules in
`story-sequences.md` (the Miro board); nothing here applies to Stories. **Bryan's rules in
`platforms.md` win wherever they disagree with this file.**

## Reels

**Cadence:** per `platforms.md` (currently 5 a week). Quality over quantity: one weak post can
undo months of trust.

**The weekly mix comes from `platforms.md`:**
- 2 results (a real job or campaign number; job-site footage beats dashboards)
- 2 teaching (one contractor problem each, tied to a belief shift)
- 1 behind the scenes (building a client's ad, site or tracking)

**Rules:**
- **Reels don't convert.** They bring the right contractors to the profile and warm them up.
  - Give them **no direct offer CTA**: the CTA is "follow for breakdowns" (or the comment keyword
    in the caption, per `platforms.md`).
  - Offers live in Stories.
- **Disqualification hooks.** Use trade language (estimates, tear-offs, slow season, crews, service
  area) so contractors feel at home and everyone else scrolls past. Don't chase viral reach or
  vanity followers.
- **Undocumented over documented.** Show the work instead of sitting down to "3 tips": open Ads
  Manager, walk through a real campaign, film on the job site.
- **Keep naming the mechanism** (the lead system / 2-step close), directly and indirectly.
- **Never mix accounts.** No car or lifestyle reels on the contractor account (`platforms.md`).

## YouTube

**Why:** Instagram grabs attention; **YouTube converts it**. 20–30 minutes with Bryan builds
more trust than any viral reel, and buyers almost always watch at least one video before they close.

**Cadence:** per `platforms.md` (the system wants one long-form a week). Saturday's Story points at it.

**The 4 formats (rotate them):**

| Format | Example |
|---|---|
| Framework / tutorial / step-by-step (converts best) | Full lead-form ad setup walkthrough for a roofer |
| Behind the results | Podcast-style chat with a contractor client: before → bridge → after |
| A → B journey | From zero leads to a booked-out month, every step |
| Who you are / where you're going | Agency life, client visits, the mission (or the personal account, per the test in `platforms.md`) |

## How they fit together

Follower ads (ICP followers about $2–6, profile visits under $0.40) + reels → profile → Stories →
YouTube → DMs → call → close. Pull, not push: the content should do about 80% of the selling.

**Trust math:**
- About **7 hours** of watch time, **11 touch points** and **4 platforms** before a high-ticket buy.
- About **100 days** to compound.
- Log where each client came from on the onboarding form.

## Gaps to flag

- A reel row carrying a direct offer CTA.
- A week whose reel mix drifts from the `platforms.md` mix.
- No long-form planned for the week (if `platforms.md` sets a weekly cadence).
- The same YouTube format three weeks running.
