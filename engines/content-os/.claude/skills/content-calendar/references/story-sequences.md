# Story Sequences (Instagram Stories only)

How `content-calendar` plans **Stories**. This follows the Story SOP board (the Miro board) and nothing else.
Reels and YouTube have their own, separate rules in `reels-youtube.md`; nothing in that file applies
here, and nothing here applies to reels.

The frames themselves are built by the `story-sequence` skill in the private `story-sequences`
repo. The default CTA is DM "LEADS" to book a meeting (see `backbone/offer.md`).

## The Story week

Every day gets one Story sequence, as one `calendar.md` row with platform `IG Story`.

| Day | Sequence | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Client result + mechanism breakdown | C | Start the week with proof and set the authority frame. Use the best contractor result (or Bryan's own) from the last 30 days, and break down every number. |
| **Tue** | Belief breaking | D | Destroy this week's objection before it comes up on a call. **Rotate objection sequences A → B → C → D**, one per week. |
| **Wed** | Value asset / lead funnel drop | A or B | Give away a checklist, SOP clip or mini training, limited to the first 50 replies. Starts new DM conversations. *(soft CTA)* |
| **Thu** | Client result #2 + before/after | C | Stack more proof. Use the before → after (identity bridge) format, so they feel behind for not working with Bryan yet. |
| **Fri** | **Hard CTA**: an irresistible offer | B | Open a set number of real spots with a real deadline, and show proof of demand as the DMs come in. Friday converts best because people reflect over the weekend. *(the one hard CTA)* |
| **Sat** | Authority / personal win | — | Show momentum: a business win or milestone, tied to "this happened because the system ran while I was doing ___". *(soft CTA → YouTube)* |
| **Sun** | Proximity / faith / lifestyle | E | Be human. **Zero pitch, no CTA.** On the contractor account, keep it to the crew, the job site and the work behind the scenes (`platforms.md`). |

**Weekly budget:**
- **3 CTAs:** 2 soft (Wed's asset drop and Sat → YouTube) and **1 hard (Fri)**.
- **Production:** 5 Stories as simple in-app Instagram edits, and **2 high-quality edits** (the Friday hard CTA plus one proof day).

## The 5 sequence types (slide structures)

| Type | Name | Slides |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Curiosity / pattern interrupt | Big-number result as the hook → why/how mechanism → DM-keyword CTA |
| **B** | FOMO / urgency / scarcity | Spot-filling claim → screenshot of DMs or numbers (proof the demand is real) → limited-access CTA (to the asset or the offer) |
| **C** | Credibility / social proof (highest converting) | Win → mechanism → proof #1 → proof #2 → stacked proof → scarcity CTA. Stack the proof, show before/after, name the mechanism |
| **D** | Belief shifting | Name the belief → throw a rock at it → reframe → prove → CTA |
| **E** | Trust / authenticity | Personal moment → values / faith → no CTA |

## The 4 objection sequences (Tuesday, rotate weekly)

Each one follows the Type D arc: **Hook → Rock → Reframe → Proof → DM keyword**. Proof is always a
real contractor result with real numbers.

| Week | Objection | Angle | Keyword |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | "I'll figure out marketing myself" | Going it alone costs months of wasted spend, and jobs lost while testing | SYSTEM |
| B | "I tried an agency before and it didn't work" | It probably wasn't their fault: leads with no qualify-and-close system don't become jobs | DIFFERENT |
| C | "I need to think about it" | Every week of waiting, competitors book the homeowners in their area | READY |
| D | "I can't afford it right now" | Do the math: one extra job a month covers it. What does staying put cost? | MATH |

## Rules

- **Never invent results, spot counts or deadlines.** If Friday has no real offer and limit, flag it
  under `## Needs You` instead of writing fake scarcity.
- Each CTA is **one DM keyword in caps**.
- Plan the proof (screenshots, numbers) and photos with the copy, so the Stories are quick to post on the day.

## Gaps to flag

- A day with no Story row.
- No hard CTA on Friday, or a second hard CTA anywhere else.
- Tuesday's objection repeating last week's (break the A → D rotation).
- A Sunday row with a CTA.
- A Monday or Thursday row with no real result attached.
