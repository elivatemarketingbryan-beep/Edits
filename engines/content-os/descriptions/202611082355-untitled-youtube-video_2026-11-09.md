Summary ⤵️

You're getting leads, driving out to projects, and after the estimate you get ghosted. Most contractors assume their ads don't work. They do. It's your sales process.

Most sales advice is written for salespeople. This is for contractors who are great at the work and were never taught how to sell it. So I treat every customer like a package: verify the order, check the quality, pack the offer right, track it, and hand it off. Skip a step and it never arrives.

The step almost every contractor skips is the follow-up, and that's exactly where the ghosting happens. If your leads live in your text messages, you don't have a pipeline. You have a pile. I show you how leads move through a CRM, stage by stage, with follow-ups that go out automatically.

But fast follow-up to the wrong person is still a waste of time. So I walk through the questions that separate a qualified lead from a tire kicker: project type, start date, budget, and who makes the decision. Then how to use those answers to decide who gets your time first, and who isn't worth driving out for yet.

Last is pricing. The first number you say decides the sale, so I break down price anchoring and why a $29,000 quote feels reasonable when a $40,000 option comes first. You'll also get the levers that protect your price, and what to do when someone says "that's too expensive" without cutting your number.
